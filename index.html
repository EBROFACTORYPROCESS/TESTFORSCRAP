Batch comparison — Planned vs Executed

Cross-checks Inventory & Stock (planned qty, estimated dates, classification stage) against Production Monitoring (actual VINs, milestone timestamps), joined on batch number. Plan-only batches have no production started yet; Exec-only batches have VINs in the tracking file but no matching row in the batch/shipment plan.
